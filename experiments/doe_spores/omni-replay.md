# Addressed experts and sensory replay

This protocol continues the parliament from the [24-turn visual experience](omni-long.md).
Its final version-4 portable spore becomes the common parent of three histories:
serial waking plus replay, worker waking plus replay, and waking without new
sleep. All three begin with the same six living experts and six recorded text
dreams. Import adopts their identities from that original archive before the
first new learning turn.

## The encounter and its replay

Turns 25–28 use Q8_0, 29–32 use F16, and 33–36 return to Q8_0. Each four-turn
block presents the same ordered encounters: left-red picture, spoken key,
left-blue picture, spoken bell. The two pictures have identical geometry. The
recordings say “The lighthouse keeper carries a key.” and the corresponding
sentence with “bell.” The native towers encode each receiving body's input.

Both learning arms save the assembled IDs, sparse media rows and text-output
policy before waking generation. After that generation's consolidation, each
arm reloads its spore in a new process and privately replays the captured input.
Waking allows 16 choices and replay 12; stop tokens end each episode earlier.
The same arithmetic uses learning rates 0.01 while awake and 0.0025 during replay.
No new anchor-cue dreams are scheduled in this continuation.

At turns 28, 32 and 36, fresh discarded probes read the spore immediately before
the last replay, immediately after it, and from the parallel waking-only history.
Both probes use the Omni text chat template with no media attached:

- `Earlier you saw a picture. Which color was on the left? Reply with one color.`
  The scored surfaces are `Red` and `Blue`, IDs 15684 and 30965.
- `Which object did the lighthouse keeper carry? Reply with one noun.`
  The scored surfaces are `key` and `bell`, IDs 4016 and 12946.

Each reading retains the full unsuppressed 128,000-entry F32 output row. AML
computes softmax probabilities and the selected logit difference. The measured
scope is this fixed sequence of two images, two recordings and two probes.

## Bodies and reproduction

The full 747-tensor files are native conversions of
`LiquidAI/LFM3-Omni-2B@21d75123f86c1f7ffb4bef1ea202710c4e7ebe67`.

| Body | SHA-256 |
|---|---|
| Q8_0 | `c48c75ba1106be4d5a5b51a807cfd17b0b67e4c35b003a48e0c524e386ad8765` |
| F16 | `bf4bf6d32737ae99e092def56dcb0236fae426a9abf5b8c9dd2ccf37a3583e01` |

The run uses AMD EPYC 9V74, GCC 14.2.0, the native AVX2/FMA/F16C paths, the
automatic packed-matrix pool and `NT_ATTN_THREADS=1`. There is no BLAS in this
build. AML is revision `78caa5b15f732d7fb83c950e544c58d23735a615`. Inference,
media encoding, elections, Oja updates, persistence and replay execute in C;
AML owns the schedule, process calls and score calculations.

```sh
make notorch notorch_omni doe_probe AML_ROOT="$AML_REPO" \
  BLAS_FLAGS= BLAS_LIBS= BLAS_NAME=native

# All supplied paths are absolute. OUT must be a new directory.
"$AML_REPO/runner/aml" experiments/doe_spores/omni_replay.aml <<EOF
$NOTORCH_ROOT/notorch_omni
$NOTORCH_ROOT/notorch
$NOTORCH_ROOT/doe_probe
$OMNI_Q8
$OMNI_F16
$IMAGE_FIXTURES
$AUDIO_FIXTURES
$PREVIOUS_RUN/sync-24-night.gguf
24
$OUT
EOF
```

`IMAGE_FIXTURES` contains `left-red.ppm` and `left-blue.ppm`, generated by
[`omni_fixtures.c`](omni_fixtures.c). `AUDIO_FIXTURES` contains the two spoken
WAVs from the native Omni reference fixture. Input episodes have exact-host
content addresses: re-encoding the same encounter after a weight change makes
the receiving body's snapshot. Returning to Q8 reproduces the original Q8
episode bytes while the population continues from its latest learned state.

The raw archive preserves the input fixtures, parent spore, generations,
episodes, output rows, traces and execution logs. A first bring-up run stopped
after one waking turn because the protocol attempted arithmetic on its textual
stdin turn count. The corrected protocol parses decimal digits; the completed
experiment starts again from the original parent in a fresh output directory.
The orchestration session became unavailable after all 12 new generations and
the first two final probes had been saved. The six final readings were rerun
from the three saved turn-36 states in a fresh directory. Both previously saved
128,000-entry rows matched their restarted readings exactly. The raw archive
retains the partial log, the separate reading protocol and both copies.

## Results

All 12 waking boundaries, all 12 replay boundaries and all 12 paired input
captures match byte for byte between serial and worker arms. At turn 36 the
population has six living identities, 3,348 waking elections, the prior six
text dreams (36 choices, 42 elections), and 12 sensory replays (66 choices,
918 prefill positions, 972 elections, 12 stops). The text-cue mask remains
5,505,094. All six legacy identities retain their adoption address, source host
and observed turn 24 through both changes of body.

Entries are differences in selected log odds, computed from the archived
score rows. At turn 36 the full-row maximum absolute changes from the last
replay are 0.263003349 and 0.258865379. At turn 32 they are 0.000011921 and
0.000012875. All six pairs retain their first greedy token. The independent
tensor audit finds writes only to elected seats' A/B and cursors; other
tensors, lineage and waking clocks retain their pre-replay bytes.

The last Q8 identity intervention reproduces all 256,000 output values of the
post-replay population exactly. Six plain baseline probes—two pictures and one
recording on each quantization—also reproduce all 768,000 logits and their
answers after the implementation change. The baseline entry and expert code
were rebuilt from `bb18d6a` against the unchanged decoder arithmetic.

The original version-4 parent imports with all eleven tensor payloads unchanged,
including its portable frame. A Q8 captured episode is refused on F16 without
publishing a child spore. When Q8 receives its own four encounters again in
the third block, each captured episode matches its first-block bytes.

## Inspect and intervene

```sh
./doe_probe lineage "$OMNI_Q8" "$OUT/sync-replay-12.gguf"

# Use the full expert addresses printed above, not current slot numbers.
./doe_probe transplant-id "$OMNI_Q8" \
  "$OUT/sync-awake-12.gguf" "$OUT/sync-replay-12.gguf" \
  factors-by-identity.gguf "$ID0" "$ID1" "$ID2" "$ID3" "$ID4" "$ID5"

cc -O2 -std=gnu11 -pthread -I. experiments/doe_spores/omni_audit.c \
  libnotorch.a -lm -o omni_audit
./omni_audit replay "$OUT/sync-awake-12.gguf" "$OUT/sync-replay-12.gguf" \
  "$OUT/sync-replay-12.trace" turn36
./omni_audit clocks "$OUT/sync-replay-12.gguf" turn36
```

The identity intervention preserves the recipient's clocks and genealogy while
copying each named expert's A/B. The independent replay audit checks the elected
seats' writes and cursors, preservation of all other tensors and the lineage,
unchanged waking clocks and text-dream mask, and the recorded replay choices
and elections.

Native regression gates are `make check_expert_lineage check_media_replay`.
They cover founder/birth identities, divergent descendants, retirement and slot
reuse, legacy 1–4 adoption, portable projection and return, malformed records,
owned worker input, sparse rows across prefill boundaries, rollback, and 96
restarted waking/replay cycles interleaved with 33 text dreams. An ignored-media
mutant fails the changed-row learning comparison; allowing an unseated living
ancestor fails the lineage reader gate.
